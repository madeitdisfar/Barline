Free and open source. Built for Windows 11.

Seamless Integration with the Taskbar

Barline paints no background at all. The taskbar's own material shows through it, so it stays right through every theme, accent and transparency change without copying any of them.

It moves with the taskbar too. Change your scaling, turn on auto-hide, open something fullscreen, and it follows along without getting in the way.

Live Visualizer

The visualizer reads what your computer is actually playing, and displays it real-time.

Four, five or six bars, coloured by your Windows accent, by the album art, or by a colour you pick. Colors are automatically adjusted to be visible against the taskbar.

Lyrics Display

Lyrics are displayed in a floating panel above the taskbar, with room for a full line and a look you choose.

Or inside the widget itself, in place of the track until you hover it.

Lyrics are pulled from LRCLIB, free and open lyrics library. Have better timings? Drop in your own .lrc file and it wins.

Yours to Style

Font, size, weight, colour, effects, background, corner radius. Make it look however you like, and every change applies the moment you make it.

Save a style as a preset file, and load it again from settings whenever you want it back.

It Keeps to Itself

No telemetry, no account, and no network at all unless lyrics are on. When they are, a lookup sends the title and artist and nothing else. Everything Barline saves stays on your computer.
