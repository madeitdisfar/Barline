Keeping Barline Going

Barline is free and open source. Every release is on GitHub, and you can compile it yourself, either of which gives you the whole app with no restriction.

The Store version is the easy way to install it and keep it updated. A few optional extras are unlocked by a single one-time purchase. Every core feature stays free and unrestricted.

Buying it is never required, but they are a great support for the development of the project. Thank you for your support!

Before You Start

Barline needs Windows 11, and it sits where the Widgets button does. Turn that off first, under Settings, then Personalization, then Taskbar, then Taskbar items.

Once it is running, right-click the widget for its settings. The same menu is on the icon in the notification area.
